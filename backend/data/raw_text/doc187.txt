Supplemental Instruction
Supplemental Instruction (SI) provides free, collaborative,
group study sessions for students taking historically difficult
courses. These sessions are hosted by an SI leader who has
previously taken the course and excelled. SI sessions
encourage active learning, critical thinking, and group
problem solving through engaging activities.
Attendance at SI sessions is free and voluntary, no
registration required! Data shows that students that
consistently attend SI sessions (5+) report higher grade
point averages than those that do not attend.
Announcements about SI are made in SI-supported courses
during the first week of classes and throughout the semester
in SI specific course shells. SI sessions begin the third week
of classes and continue throughout the semester.

Tips to Succeed in Supplemental
Instruction
Bring your lecture materials, textbook, and/or notes to your SI
sessions to help you when you get stuck.
SI Leaders aren’t lecturers, so be ready to participate and review
the materials from class.
You will be working collaboratively with other students from their
course and will discover new ways to process and study the
information in an engaging and welcoming environment.
SI is most beneficial when attended regularly. On average, students
that attend SI for 5 or more sessions see a 0.5 increase in their
grade compared to students who don’t attend SI!
