Bursar Office
The Bursar’s Office is responsible for the assessment, billing and collection of tuition and fees at The University of Texas at
Dallas.
Our mission is to manage all financial transactions involving students, faculty and staff with efficiency and accuracy, while
consistently delivering respectful and exceptional customer service.
We hope the information provided on this website helps you better understand the functions of the Bursar’s Office and
supports students in meeting their financial responsibilities at UT Dallas.
Please note: The Bursar’s Office operates virtually and is not available for in-person transactions.
We encourage you to take advantage of our online services to make payments and access important information. If you need
assistance, we’re here to help.
Thank you for visiting, and we look forward to assisting you!
