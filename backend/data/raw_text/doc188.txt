About the Testing Center
The UT Dallas Testing Center is a 300-seat computer lab that offers a comfortable and secure testing environment for online,
paper, and hybrid exams. Our facility provides services in accordance with the National College Testing Association
(NCTA) Professional Standards and Guidelines. The UT Dallas Testing Center currently administers the following exams:
For Current UT Dallas Students Only:
Institutional exams for UT Dallas courses
TSIA2 Assessment
ALEKS Math Placement
Computer Science Placement
Certification Exams:
Licensed Child Care Administrator (LCCA) Exam
Licensed Child Placing Agency (LCPA) Exam
Distance Learning/Correspondence Testing:
Exam Proctoring for non-UT Dallas courses
