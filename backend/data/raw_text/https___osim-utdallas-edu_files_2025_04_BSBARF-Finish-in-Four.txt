Notes: 
1. MATH 1325 is required for all JSOM undergraduate students. Students may substitute MATH 2413 or MATH 2417 for MATH 1325. 
To make timely degree progress, students should complete MATH 1325 or its equivalent by the end of their first semester at UT  
Dallas. Students who will not meet this requirement should contact their academic advisor to discuss their degree timeline.  
2. Real Estate Finance Major Related Courses: Students must take REAL 3305, REAL 3358, REAL 3365, REAL 4321, REAL 4328 . 
Final 3 semester credit hours any FIN upper-division course not used elsewhere on degree plan. Note that completing REAL 4328 
required for this concentration satisfies a capstone requirement.  
3. An Internship (Practicum) of at least 160 working hours is required for all JSOM majors. Enroll in BA 4090 at the time of internship 
for 0 credit hours to fulfill degree requirement only. Students who are employed in full -time jobs may fulfill their intern ship 
requirement with their current employer as a special project for registration in BA 4090 with preapproval from their program director. 
BA 4090 does NOT need to be completed in spring of year three but must be completed in order to graduate. Students c an move 
internship to any semester, including summer.  
4. A Community Engagement Experience is required for all JSOM majors in fall 2018 catalog or later. Students may fulfill requirement 
with BPS 4396 or ENTP 4340 or MKT 4360 for 3 semester credit hours JSOM Major Core, or with BA 4095 upon completion of 100 
service hours for 0-hour degree requirement only. If completing BA 4095, student must complete 3 hours of free elective credit to 
satisfy the total of 120 semester credit hours - 45 semester credit hours of which must be upper division - required for graduati on. 
5. BPS 4395 Prerequisites: Junior or Senior standing and ACCT 2301, FIN 3320, ITSS 3300, HMGT 3301, MKT 3300, OPRE 3310, 
IMS 3310, OBHR 3310, or OBHR 3330. Prerequisite or Corequisite: BCOM 3300  
6. BPS 4305 Prerequisites: BA 1105 or BA 3105 and ACCT 2301, FIN 3320, HMGT 3301, IMS 3310, ITSS 3300, MKT 3300, OBHR 
3310, OBHR 3330, or OPRE 3310.
