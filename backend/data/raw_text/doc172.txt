Academic Success
Coaching
Need to improve your study habits?
Academic Success Coaching is the one-on-one process of helping
students devise a personalized approach to studying. This
program serves as a general resource, where students can receive
help with various school-related topics such as:
Time management
Prioritizing assignments/tasks
Effective study strategies
Note-taking strategies
Goal setting
Study environment tips
Test taking tips
Academic Success Coaching offers in-person appointments for
currently enrolled undergraduate students. If you would like to
partner with an Academic Success Coach to discuss any of the ASC
topics, please visit Accudemia to schedule an appointment today!
Please note: If you are seeking academic support and are not sure
where to start, a good place to visit is the Student Success Center.
We will be happy to help you or refer you to the many campus
resources that are here to support you in your academic journey.
