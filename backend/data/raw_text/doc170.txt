12twenty@UTDallas for Students
12twenty@UTDallas is The University of Texas at Dallas’ career services platform for connecting Comets to career opportunities.
Through 12twenty, you can:
Register for career events and career fairs
Schedule career advising, resume, and job search appointments
Search and apply for jobs and internships
Apply for on-campus student employment opportunities

How to Get Started
Log In to Your Student/Alumni Account
If you are a current student, log in today using your UT Dallas Single Sign-On (SSO). Please do not create a new account.
LOG IN – UT DALLAS SSO
If you are alumni, please reach out to 12twentySupport@utdallas.edu to activate your account.

Getting started only takes a few minutes. Once logged in, complete the recommended profile steps to receive the most relevant
opportunities and resources for you.
Reminders
Appointments, jobs, and events are now being managed through 12twenty only. The last day to access Handshake to gather any
of your application materials is August 31, 2026.
If you have any questions, please reach out to 12twentySupport@utdallas.edu for assistance.
