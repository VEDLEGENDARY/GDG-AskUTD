The School of Interdisciplinary Studies advising team is committed to assisting you in the development of an educational
plan that is compatible with your career goals. Academic advisors work with you to provide you with the resources you
need to successfully complete your academic degree. Advising is an important part of a successful college experience. To
ensure that you get the most out of your advising appointment, it is recommended that you take an active part in your
degree planning. Interdisciplinary Studies advisors can assist you with:
Degree plan requirements

Course selection
Changing your major/minor
Transfer credit approval
Applying for graduation
Graduation audit
Understanding University policies
Referrals to other UTD resources
As a student, you are responsible for:
Meeting with your advisor each semester
Reviewing course offerings
Sharing courses you want to take with your advisor
Contacting your advisor with other questions about your degree
