Peer Tutoring
Peer Tutoring is a drop-in tutoring service offered to currently
enrolled students in various math, chemistry, physics, and
engineering courses. As a drop-in tutoring service, you do not need
to schedule an appointment in advance; rather, you can simply
show up to the tutoring lab any time we are open for some help.
Our tutoring lab is located in the bottom floor of the McDermott
Library in room MC 1.304.
Check the schedules below to see which courses we offer tutoring
for, what times tutoring is available for each course, and the
number of tutors we have on for each course at any given time.
