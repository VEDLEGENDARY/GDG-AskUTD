UT Dallas offers financial assistance for qualified students and a
variety of other tools that families can use to make higher
education possible. Nearly 70 percent of UT Dallas undergraduates
receive some sort of need- or merit-based financial aid.

Comet Promise: Full Tuition & Fee
Coverage
Eligible freshman and transfer applicants with a family
income of $100,000 or less will have their full tuition
covered through a combination of the Comet Promise
funding, scholarships, grants and other tuition exemption
programs.
