Transfer Scholarships
Transfer students are considered for scholarship awards
based on incoming transferrable hours, GPA, academic
qualification and/or membership in select organizations.
Comet Transfer Scholarship
Transfer applicants are reviewed automatically for the Comet
Transfer Scholarship when admitted. To be considered, students
must be a Texas resident at the time of admission and have
between 30–90 transferrable credit hours. As credit is petitioned
after admission, credit by examination (such as AP/IB/CLEP) is not
considered as part of an applicant’s transferable GPA or transfer
hours for admissions.

Transfer Academic Achievement
Scholarship
This scholarship is designed for high-achieving transfer students,
and applicants are encouraged to complete the application process
by the financial aid priority deadline for the Fall application term.
Scholarships are limited, awarded competitively and based on the
caliber of each incoming transfer class.

Comet Scholars Program
The Comet Scholars Program awards scholarships to both
freshman and transfer students who have a record of academic
excellence in high school or community college and show
significant financial need. Recipients of this scholarship also receive
academic support from program staff to assist them in successfully
transitioning to UT Dallas.

Additional Scholarship Opportunities
UT Dallas awards millions of dollars in scholarships each year to
incoming students. Some awards are automatic, while many
require an application and are offered through a variety of sources.
Visit our Financial Aid website to learn more about additional
scholarship opportunities.
