Comets LANding
Comets LANding is our community Gaming & Esports
Center located in the heart of campus; across from the Founders
Building and adjacent to our Student Union. Comets LANding is
open to all members of the UT Dallas community. With two floors
encompassing 16,000 square feet, numerous PCs and popular
console areas, there are opportunities for gamers of all skill levels.
