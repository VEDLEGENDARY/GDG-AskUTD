The University of Texas at Dallas values its students. We are committed to the success of each and every one. Professional
academic advising is an important tool to help our students reach their goals.
Advisors provide students with valuable guidance on:
Degree plans and options.
Specific departmental emphases and faculty research interests.
How best to access and interface with faculty and instructors.
Issues including class suitability, degree requirements, University policies and procedures, study skills, time management,
campus involvement and limited personal issues.
Required and elective options.
How to apply credit by examination and transfer credits.
Ensuring their degree and graduation requirements are met.
Students have access to advisors at any time, but should make an appointment to meet with their advisor at least once each
semester.
