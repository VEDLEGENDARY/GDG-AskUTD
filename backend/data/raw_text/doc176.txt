Comet Treks
The UCC Comet Treks is a unique program designed to enhance the
educational and professional development of students by
facilitating employer site visits. Comet Treks offer students the
chance to explore potential career paths, network with
professionals, and gain a broader understanding of various
industries.
A typical Comet Trek includes:
A tour of the company
Presentation or information session
Panel discussion
Q&A session
Networking opportunities with UTD alumni and industry
professionals
Comet Treks are posted on 12twenty.

Student Success Stories
Hasan Iqbal – Emerson
Mechanical Engineering
“I had the opportunity to attend Comet Trek in the Fall of
2024 and visit Emerson in McKinney. As a Mechanical
Engineering student in ECS undergrad, Emerson was
one of the companies I wanted to apply for an
internship/Co-Op.
“During the visit, I was able to introduce myself and
connect with engineers working at Emerson directly,
and ask a ton of questions about the company and roles
offered. After applying for a Co-Op role, I was able to
easily interview with the company because they already
knew me from the visit, which resulted in getting hired
for a Co-Op the following year!”
Hitesh Kodavatikanti – CBRE
Business Analytics & AI

“Attending the Comet Trek to the CBRE Richardson office
was truly a transformative experience for me. Before
the trek, I had very limited knowledge about what CBRE
does and the commercial real estate industry as a
whole. But this visit gave me valuable insight into the
company’s operations, culture, and the broader industry
landscape.
“During the session, we were introduced to the Digital &
Technology Summer Internship program and were given
a clear overview of the program structure and timeline.
That experience sparked my interest and gave me the
confidence to apply.
“I’m excited to share that I was shortlisted and later
offered a position for the summer internship—and I truly
believe that Comet Trek played a huge role in securing
this opportunity. During my interview, I was able to
speak about my experience during the trek, and it
clearly demonstrated my interest and passion for both
CBRE and the commercial real estate industry.”
Kunuth Siddiqui – Trinity River Authority
Software Engineering

“Attending multiple Comet Treks, including visits to
Trinity River Authority, Motorola Solutions, and other
organizations, has been an incredibly valuable
experience for me. Each trek gave me insight into the
company’s mission, culture, and the wide range of

connect directly with professionals and learn from their

“After attending the Trinity River Authority trek, I was
fortunate to be hired as an intern there, which made the

“I am truly grateful to the University Career Center for
organizing these opportunities, as they provide students
with firsthand exposure that can genuinely shape their
career paths and open doors to future success.”
