Academic advisors are assigned to students based on a student’s major and last name (see Undergraduate Advisors below).
Natural Sciences and Mathematics (NSM) Academic Advisors assist undergraduate students via UTD email, virtual appointments
and in-person appointments. We recommend all undergraduate students meet with their academic advisor at least once per
semester.
Reasons to set-up an appointment include:
Review degree progress audit and degree plan
Discuss a semester by semester plan toward graduation
Discuss change of major to NSM or within NSM
Discuss adding a minor
The NSM Advising Suite in Classroom Building (CB 1.101) will be open Monday through Thursday from 9:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m. and
2:00 p.m. to 5:00 p.m.
Only virtual appointments will be scheduled on Fridays.
Location: Classroom Building (CB 1.101) East of PS3
If you need to reach the NSM Advising Office by phone, 972-883-2500.

Undergraduate Advisors
Find your assigned academic advisor below, based on our major and the first letter(s) of your last name (unless “All”
is indicated). Current students may schedule an appointment with your assigned academic advisor via your Polaris
Student Portal. Polaris is a new tool designed to make connecting with academic advisors and support services
easier than ever. For information on how to schedule an appointment visit Polaris Student Portal. (Under Current
UT Dallas Students).
Thinking about changing your major?
Students wishing to change their major into or within NSM should email the academic advisor assigned to the major
they are interested in. Please do not use Polaris to book an appointment for major changes.
Out of consideration for others, please reschedule if you will be unable to make it to your appointment.
