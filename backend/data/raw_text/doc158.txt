Abbreviation Description
####
Used in the transfer credit tool as the sending institution’s course
number to indicate all courses for a given prefix/department abbreviation
have the same equivalency
GNED Used in the transfer credit tool as an indication that the External Course
carries a specific Core Curriculum requirement code to UT Dallas
LLUC 1— Generic lower-division (freshman) credit
LLUC 2— Generic lower-division (sophomore) credit
ULUC 3— Generic upper-division (junior) credit
ULUC 4— Generic upper-division (senior) credit
UTD XXXX The course has been determined to be transferable to UT Dallas and a
specific equivalency will be determined after enrollment
College/Course Not Found
If a specific school or course is missing from the transfer credit
tool, it does not necessarily mean that the coursework will not
transfer to UT Dallas. Instead, it indicates that the coursework’s
equivalency is unknown and will be determined during an official
transfer evaluation.
Search by UTD Course
Look up courses from UT Dallas. View their transfer equivalencies at other colleges or universities.

Official/Unofficial Transfer Evaluation
Official transfer evaluations are conducted for current UT Dallas
students after receipt of an official transcript as of the admissions
process. The transfer credit tool can only provide you with an
unofficial transfer evaluation. Transferability of any listed course is
not guaranteed.
Science and Math Courses
Course sequences vary among institutions and may not directly
equate with UT Dallas sequences on a course-for-course basis.
Common examples of this include Biology, Calculus, and Chemistry.
Credit Disclaimer
UT Dallas awards transfer credit according to the policies listed in
the UT Dallas course catalogs.
For incoming transfer students, the transfer credit tool results may
be awarded after submitting an admissions application and
transcripts.
For current UT Dallas students, please consult your academic
advisor before taking a course at UTD or another institution.
