Supportive Guides
As a member of the UT Dallas community, you may be the ﬁrst one to notice
a student exhibiting distress, disruptive behavior or academic dishonesty. The
student does not need to be violating any University rules or regulations for
you to be concerned. Please contact the Ofﬁce of Community Standards and
Conduct and we can begin a dialogue with the student and provide referrals.
Allegations of student conduct violations are to be referred to the Dean of Students.

Behavior Assessment and Intervention Team
The Behavior Assessment and Intervention Team (BAIT) reviews behavioral incidents and ensures a
systematic response to students whose behavior may be disruptive or harmful to themselves or the
UT Dallas community, and assists in promoting the health, safety and welfare of students and other
members of the UT Dallas community.
