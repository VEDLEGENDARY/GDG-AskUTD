Schedule Planner Validation
When preparing for registration, Schedule Planner will enable you to check if your registration will
be successful prior to your registration appointment.
To do so, once you have selected your desired schedule, click on the Validate box.
If you receive the following message, your registration should go through successfully.
If you receive a warning message, you may need to seek assistance from your advisor for
registration.
